## Talk with Maestro live

Live mode lets you have a voice conversation with Maestro, right inside your chat. It works with Gemini Live, OpenAI Realtime, or llama.cpp through your Sampler Hub, with optional transcription.

- Maestro's voice color can now tint the whole app and its launcher icon.
- Download voice packs with their speed, time left and a cancel button.
- New home screen widgets: start a discussion or open the camera in one tap, or show your Maestro butterfly.
- Choose and reorder the cards on the Sampler home. Your layout follows you on every device paired with your hub.
- Smoother chat scrolling and card reordering, and suggestions stack on phones.
- Opera on a computer now finds your Sampler Hub on Wi-Fi networks that hid it before.
- Opera now fits Chromebooks, Android laptops and a phone in desktop mode: a docked sidebar, a roomy layout, and controls hidden when a camera or microphone is missing.
- Download an image Maestro sends you, or copy its link, from the image preview.
- Opera is coming to iPhone and iPad, with the same chat, tools and Sampler Hub pairing.
- The Mac download is now signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a security warning.
- Search your settings, with shortcuts to pages and your recent cards, and a floating search bar in conversations.
- The update banner in settings now opens the release notes, with a button to see the release on GitHub.
- Polished settings and sheets, with clearer provider model details and roomier text fields.

This version also hardens tools and widgets: Opera asks before each MCP tool runs, and HTML, Mermaid and math widgets run apart from the app.
