## Apple's built-in model

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Opera can now use the model that ships with Apple Intelligence, right on your device. Nothing to download and nothing leaves your device.

- It needs iOS 26 or macOS 26 on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- On iOS 27 and macOS 27, it can also read the images you send, and the model details show which Apple model your device runs and how much text it can keep in mind.
- The iPhone and iPad build is more reliable to install, with its widgets and Sampler Hub settings working again.
